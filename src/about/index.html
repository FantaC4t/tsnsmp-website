Our Story

🌱 How We Started

Back in January 2025, an SMP we were apart of suddenly...ended. It was super sudden and unexpected, leaving us all hanging in the aftermath of the shutdown.

A lot of us drifted apart after that, but some of us stayed close. Felix- the ever so wonderful person that he is, suggested we made our own server. At first it was just a joke, "Us? Running a server?" but we sat on it for awhile and realized that there wasn't really anything stopping us from actually doing it. So we did.

Best. Idea. Ever!

What started as a small group goofing around and building random things together, turned into a bustling server we were never saw coming. Everyone here is so creative and inviting, we couldn't think of a better server to play on.

📈 Plot Twist: We Got Popular

Season 1 was wild, not gonna lie. We invited a few friends, they invited their friends, and then we had this bustling community! Don't get us wrong - we loved it. The energy was incredible, so many amazing builds, and we met some genuinely awesome people.

But if we're being honest? It got a bit overwhelming.It was hard to keep players active and some folks got parasocial with our owners. We went from knowing everyone knowing each other to . The magic of those early days where we all knew each other's building styles and inside jokes started to fade.

🏡 Season 2: Getting Our Groove Back

So in July 2025, we decided to hit the reset button - not because Season 1 was bad, but because we missed that tight-knit community vibe. Season 2 is our way of getting back to what made TSNSMP special in the first place.

We're being way pickier now (sorry not sorry), because we want every single person here to genuinely add to our little chaotic group. Quality over quantity, always! 💚
