01 The Beginning

🌱 How We Started

Back in January 2025, an SMP we were a part of suddenly ended. It was super sudden and unexpected, leaving us all hanging in the aftermath of the shutdown.

Felix — the ever so wonderful person that he is — suggested we made our own server. At first it was just a joke: "Us? Running a server?" But we sat on it for a while and realized there wasn't really anything stopping us. So we did.

Best. Idea. Ever.

02 The Growth Spurt

📈 Plot Twist: We Got Popular

Season 1 was wild. We invited a few friends, they invited their friends — and suddenly we had this bustling community! The energy was incredible, but it got a bit overwhelming. The magic of those early days started to fade.

03 The Reset

🏡 Season 2: Getting Our Groove Back

In July 2025 we hit the reset button — not because Season 1 was bad, but because we missed that tight-knit vibe. We're being way pickier now, because we want every person here to genuinely add to the group. 💚

Quality over quantity — every single time.
