What we look for in applicants:

  • ✨ A positive attitude and respect for everyone
  • 🏗️ A willingness to build, collaborate, and help others. Skill level doesnt matter! We accept EVERYONE!
  • 🚫 No griefing, drama, or toxic behavior, keep the server positively vibed and not toxic
  • 🌱 Age 18+ (younger applicants welcome if mature or vouched for) we do our best to accommodate everyone and dont want to be held responsible for more minors.
  • 🤝 Someone who wants to keep the server friendly and fun and is willing to meet new friends!
📬
