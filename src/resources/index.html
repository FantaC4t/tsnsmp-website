Server Resources

Everything you need to know about playing on TSNSMP

Server Rules

1

Follow Discord TOS

We strictly adhere to the Discord Terms of Service.

2

No Sensitive Topics

No IRL politics or religion discussions. We're here to play Minecraft.

3

Zero Tolerance for Hate

No racism, homophobia, transphobia, or any form of hate speech.

4

No NSFW Content

Keep all content appropriate for all ages. No NSFW content of any kind.

5

Fair Play

No hacking or cheating. QoL mods (like Fullbright, Minimaps) are allowed. Use common sense.

6

Consensual PvP Only

PvP is allowed only if both parties agree. "Jokingly" attacking people is not okay if they didn't consent.

7

Respect Property

No griefing and no stealing. Keep the vibes chill and respectful.

8

Stream Friendly

Keep builds and in-game content friendly. Many of our members stream their gameplay.

9

Common Sense

If it's not listed here, it doesn't mean it's okay. We expect players to use common sense.
