Server Rules

Keep it chill. Keep it fun. Keep it respectful.

1

Follow Discord TOS

We strictly adhere to the Discord Terms of Service.

2

No Sensitive Topics

Please be mindful when speaking about sensitive topics, religion, politics etc, keep drama out of the server.

3

Zero Tolerance for Hate

No racism, homophobia, transphobia, or any form of hate speech.

4

No NSFW Content

Keep all content appropriate for all ages. No NSFW content of any kind.

5

Fair Play

No hacking or cheating. QoL mods (like Fullbright, Minimaps) are allowed. Use common sense.

6

Consensual PvP Only

PvP is allowed only if both parties agree. "Jokingly" attacking people is not okay if they didn't consent.

7

Respect Property

No griefing and no stealing. Keep the vibes chill and respectful.

8

Stream Friendly

Keep builds and in-game content friendly. Many of our members stream their gameplay.

9

Common Sense

If it's not listed here, it doesn't mean it's okay. We expect players to use common sense.

Custom Mods

PlayerStatus Custom

Two handy commands made just for TSNSMP.

  • /live — Announce to the server you're going live. Set a stream link with /live link <url>.
  • /color <hexcode> — Color your name with any RGB hex color.
  • /nosleep — Toggle your status to "no sleep". When enabled, other players are notified if you try to sleep, and a message is sent when you toggle it.

Quality of Life

AudioPlayer

Put custom sounds on discs, goat horns and note blocks.

ClickVillagers

Shift + right-click a villager to pick it up. Place it back by placing the villager head — works in boats and minecarts too.

Kiss Mod

Kiss another player.

  • /kiss <player>
Graves

A grave spawns where you die, keeping your items safe.

Instant Groups

Pull everyone nearby into a Simple Voice Chat open group instantly.

  • /instantgroup <range>
Image2Map

Render any image onto maps. Each map is 128×128 px. The URL must point directly to an image file.

  • /image2map create <W> <H> <dither/none> <url>
RealHeight

Change your player height. Limited to 1.2 – 2.2 blocks.

  • /trigger height_menu
Peek Client mod needed

Shows an item preview on shulker boxes without opening them.

Replanting Crops

Break crops with a hoe and they replant themselves automatically.

MasterCutter

500+ new stonecutter recipes — woodcutting, stripping, recycling and more.

FormatName

Tweak name colors and add effects to your items.

  • /trigger FormatName
NoWeatherSkip

Sleeping no longer skips rain/thunder — weather plays out naturally.

Mobs & Creatures

Happy Ghast Stopper

Rename a harness to STOP (case-sensitive) and equip it on a Happy Ghast to stop it from wandering.
